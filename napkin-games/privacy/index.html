Privacy Policy

Napkin Games · Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version: Napkin Games collects nothing. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn’t connect to the internet.

What the app keeps

A few things are saved on your device so the app works the way you left it: your sound and haptics settings, the options you last picked for each game, your best Guess the Doodle scores, the last few words it dealt (so they don't come up again too soon), the monsters you stick on the fridge in Fold-a-Monster, and a count of finished rounds that decides when to ask for an App Store rating. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Drawings and Photos

Doodles and monsters never leave your device unless you share one yourself with the Share button. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks your permission to add it to Photos. Napkin Games can only add pictures and can’t see your library. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Sharing with others

We don’t share or sell any data, because the app doesn’t collect any. There are no third-party analytics tools, ad networks or SDKs in the app.

Apple

Apple handles your purchase and downloads under Apple’s privacy policy. If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can turn that off anytime.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We don’t add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone. Ask, and we’ll delete our emails with you.

Children

Napkin Games is fine for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page before the app changes, along with the date at the top.

Contact
