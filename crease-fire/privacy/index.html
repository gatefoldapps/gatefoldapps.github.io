Privacy Policy

Crease Fire · Last updated October 2026

The short version: Crease Fire collects nothing. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn’t connect to the internet.

What the app keeps

A few things are saved on your device so the app works the way you left it: your sound, music, sea and haptics settings, the options and colors you last picked for a match, a match you haven’t finished yet, the ships you keep in My Dock, the captain names you type (with each one’s colors, record and stickers), your wins and losses against the computer, your Voyage stars and win streak, and a count of finished matches that decides when to ask for an App Store rating. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing with others

We don’t share or sell any data, because the app doesn’t collect any. There are no third-party analytics tools, ad networks or SDKs in the app.

Apple

Apple handles your purchase and downloads under Apple’s privacy policy. If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can turn that off anytime.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We don’t add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone. Ask, and we’ll delete our emails with you.

Children

Crease Fire is fine for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page before the app changes, along with the date at the top.

Contact

Crease Fire is made by Gatefold. Questions about privacy: gatefoldapps@gmail.com
