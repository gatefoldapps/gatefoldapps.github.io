Privacy Policy

Crease Fire · Last updated November 2026

The short version: Crease Fire has no ads, analytics or tracking. Local play works offline. Optional online play connects to Apple Game Center; no separate Gatefold account is needed.

What the app keeps

A few things are saved on your device so the app works the way you left it: your sound, music, sea and haptics settings, the options and colors you last picked for a match, a match you haven’t finished yet, the ships you keep in My Dock, the captain names you type (with each one’s colors, record and stickers), your wins and losses against the computer, your Voyage stars and win streak, and a count of finished matches that decides when to ask for an App Store rating. These local settings and records stay on your device. Online match data is described below; deleting the app does not delete matches held by Game Center.

Sharing with others

We do not sell your data or use third-party analytics or advertising tools. Online play exchanges the match information described below with Apple Game Center and your opponent.

Online play

Game Center provides your player identifier, display name and available profile photo for online matches. Match rules, moves, results and carried Captain’s Papers stamps are exchanged through Game Center. Your fleet is sealed before play and revealed to the other captain at the end or with an in-game resignation so the result can be checked. Match history and fleet drawings are stored locally and in the Game Center match data as needed for play. Gatefold does not operate its own game server; match data travels through Apple Game Center and the open sea uses Apple CloudKit. Game Center is an Apple service governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

The open sea works through a public database in Apple’s CloudKit, in Gatefold’s iCloud container. When you throw a bottle, it stores your Game Center player identifier, your Game Center display name, the match rules, the day’s tide, the preset note you picked and the time. Other players who open the sea can read those details. Taking a bottle writes a small record that marks it as taken, and you can recall your own bottle to remove it. No typed messages are sent, and the open sea needs an iCloud account only to throw or take a bottle.

Apple

Apple handles your purchase and downloads under Apple’s privacy policy. If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can turn that off anytime.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We don’t add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone. Ask, and we’ll delete our emails with you.

Children

Local play requires no account. Optional online play uses Game Center and the same match information described above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page before the app changes, along with the date at the top.
